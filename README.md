# 🚀 Three-Year Plan

**Overall goal: pursue a master's degree**, with a focus on artificial intelligence / computer applications.

- **Junior year, fall**: build the foundation — first round of math (calculus / linear algebra / probability), English vocabulary and intensive reading, first round of professional courses
- **Junior year, spring**: strengthen — intensive math practice, second round of professional courses, finish one complete project (a small game or an Agent tool)
- **Senior year, fall**: sprint — mock exams and past papers, fill the gaps, and take the entrance exam in December
- **Senior year, spring**: prepare for the second-round interview (professional courses, spoken English, project presentation) and finish the graduation project, ideally related to AI or games

**Why I choose to pursue a master's degree:**

1. **To improve my educational background and job competitiveness**: with only a bachelor's degree, the positions I can choose from are limited; a master's degree raises both the starting point and the range of choices, and this is my main reason
2. **To build a solid foundation for my target directions**: positions in AI Agent and game development require a much stronger foundation in computer science (operating systems, compilers, algorithms), and I want to spend those years building it properly instead of only being able to write course assignments
3. **Interest-driven**: I want a stretch of time to do real research and real projects, and turn "I want to make games and understand Agents" from an idea into actual ability
